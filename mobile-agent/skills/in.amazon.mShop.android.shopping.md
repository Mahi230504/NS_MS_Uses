# Amazon India (in.amazon.mShop.android.shopping)

## Navigation
- Search bar at the very top; tap to open the search input.
- Bottom tabs: Home, You, Cart, Menu.

## Search and select
- For "<product>": tap the FIRST organic (non-sponsored) result. Sponsored
  results have a small "Sponsored" label — skip them in favour of the real
  best match.
- Don't open multiple product pages for a single requested item.
- On the product page, "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" — if user said "buy", use
  Buy Now; otherwise Add to Cart.

## Cart and checkout — HITL mandatory
- "Buy Now" and "Place Your Order" — need_approval with cart contents
  (and price) in reason.
- Address-selection screen on first order — HITL.
