# Amazon India (in.amazon.mShop.android.shopping)

## Navigation
- Search bar at the very top; tap to open the search input.
- Bottom tabs: Home, You, Cart, Menu.

## Pitfalls
- "Buy Now" and "Place Your Order" are HITL-worthy.
- Sponsored ads in search results may appear before the actual best match — read carefully before opening.
- Address-selection screen may interrupt checkout on first order.
