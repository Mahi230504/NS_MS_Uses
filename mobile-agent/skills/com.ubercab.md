# Uber (com.ubercab)

## Navigation
- The "Where to?" field is at the top of the home map.
- After selecting destination, ride type list appears at the bottom; "Confirm Uber" / "Confirm Auto" buttons.

## Pitfalls
- "Confirm" on the ride type is HITL-worthy — that books the ride.
- A safety/promo sheet may slide in over the map; dismiss before tapping anything.
- Location permission prompt on first launch — HITL.
